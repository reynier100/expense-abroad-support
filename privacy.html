Privacy Policy

Effective: September 23, 2026

Expense Abroad is designed to work without an account. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

Trip details, budgets, expenses, purchase scenarios, preferences, saved exchange-rate information, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device. Expense Abroad does not upload this information to a developer-operated server.

Currency-rate requests

When you request a live currency rate, the app contacts the Frankfurter currency-rate service using the source and destination currency codes. That provider may receive standard network information, such as your IP address, as part of delivering the request. Expense Abroad does not send your trip budget, expense descriptions, or purchase amounts with that request.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Expense Abroad receives purchase and entitlement status from Apple so it can unlock Premium features. We do not operate a separate purchase server and do not receive your payment-card details.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily budget reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Exports and sharing

The app creates a CSV export only when you request it. You choose where to save or share that file using Apple’s system share controls. Once shared, the receiving service’s privacy practices apply.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect personal data, sell personal data, or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. Expense Abroad does not request precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or financial-account credentials.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual trips and expenses inside the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data, subject to normal device backups managed by you and Apple.

Children’s privacy

Expense Abroad is a general-audience budgeting utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact
