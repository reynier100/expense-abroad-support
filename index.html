Quick help

Restore Premium

Open More → Expense Abroad Premium, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased Premium.

Currency rates

Open More → Currency rates to refresh the current rate. If a live rate is unavailable, the app can use a saved rate or a rate you enter manually.

Your trip data

Trips and expenses are kept locally on your device. Use Export Trip Data to create a CSV copy before removing the app or changing devices.

Daily reminder

Open More → Daily Budget Reminder. Notifications stay on your device and can be disabled at any time.

Contact support
